The Photopolarimeter-Radiometer (PPR) aboard Galileo measured thermal emission from Jupiter's Galilean moons, providing temperature maps that reveal surface thermophysical properties and geological variations.

#### Dataset Information

| Property | Value |
|----------|-------|
| **Source** | Galileo PPR |
| **Targets** | Europa, Ganymede, Callisto |
| **Type** | Thermal emission maps |
| **Wavelength** | 17–110 μm |

#### Scientific Background

Surface temperatures on icy moons are controlled by solar heating, thermal inertia, and internal heat flow. PPR observations constrain the thermal inertia of surface materials, helping to distinguish between fine-grained frost and consolidated ice. On Europa, thermal anomalies may indicate regions of recent geological activity or enhanced heat flow.

#### Dataset Contents

This dataset includes processed PPR thermal observations with:

- Calibrated brightness temperature maps
- Derived thermal inertia estimates
- Observation geometry and timing information
