The Photopolarimeter-Radiometer (PPR) aboard the Galileo spacecraft measured thermal emission from Europa and other Jovian satellites, providing constraints on surface temperatures and thermal properties.

#### Dataset Information

| Property | Value |
|----------|-------|
| **Source** | Galileo PPR |
| **Target** | Europa, Jovian satellites |
| **Type** | Thermal emission |

#### Scientific Background

PPR thermal observations of Europa reveal surface temperatures that depend on local time, latitude, and surface properties. These data help constrain the thermal inertia and heat flow of Europa's icy shell.
